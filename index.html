PAGASA Signal No. 4 Bagyong Sigwa Brgy. San Isidro, Bulacan SDG 11

Landfall was four hours ago. The spillway release has pushed floodwater through the barangay to second-storey level and it is still rising. Power is out, the streets are canals, and eight residents are on their roofs waiting for a boat. You have one rubber boat, room for three at a time, and about five minutes before the current makes these streets impassable.

Objectives

  • Sweep the flooded streets and find residents by their signal lights.
  • Pull up alongside and bring them aboard — three to a trip.
  • Run them back to the evacuation centre at the elementary school.
  • Mind the hull. Debris and walls at speed will sink you.
